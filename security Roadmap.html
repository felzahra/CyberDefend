Self-Protection Tips

Different malware serves different purposes—from stealing passwords to encrypting files or secretly monitoring victims. To protect yourself:

  • Keep your operating system and software updated.
  • Use reputable antivirus and anti-malware solutions.
  • Never download attachments or click links from unknown sources.
  • Regularly back up critical data offline to mitigate ransomware impact.
