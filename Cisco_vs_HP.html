🧠 Memory Trick — "CUA" Rule:
• Cisco and Aruba are similar (both derived from classic CLI)
• Undo is HP-only (Cisco/Aruba use "no")
• Use "system-view" for HP, "config t" for Cisco/Aruba
• Add "-static" to HP routes, "-interface" to HP VLAN interfaces
• Unique HP terms: PVID (native), Bridge-Aggregation (port-channel), edged-port (portfast), import-route (redistribute), display (show)
